Michelle Porter's real estate career began in 1994 in the Greensboro and Triad area. Over the years, she has helped individuals and families navigate important real estate decisions with attention, integrity, and personal care.

Today, her expertise also extends to Pinehurst and Moore County, connecting buyers and sellers with guidance informed by decades of professional experience and local market knowledge.

Affiliated with Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Pinehurst Realty Group and Yost & Little Realty, Michelle offers personal service supported by established brokerage resources.
